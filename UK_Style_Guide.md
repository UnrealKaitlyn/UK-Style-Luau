## Style & Case

- Use tabs for indents with a size of 4.
- Surround and separate functions around two newlines.
- Separate blocks of variables and operations (such as for loops) with a newline.

Use camelCase for:
- Local Variables & Functions

Use PascaslCase for:
- Global Variables & Functions
- Services
- Modules
- Methods

Use LOUD_SNAKE_CASE for:
- Constants

Use double quotes ("") for:
- Strings
- Printing, Warnings, Errors, Assert()

Use single quotes ('') for:
- TypeChecking
- Anything that uses Strings for identification, e.g. ['JumpPower']
